## 2. Suspicious Messages for Fraud Detection

### Scenario A: Urgent KYC Suspension Threat (Critical Risk)
```text
Dear customer, your SBI bank account will be blocked today due to pending KYC. Verify your PAN and Aadhaar immediately at http://sbi-kyc-update.top/login or contact customer care at +919876543210.
```

### Scenario B: Unsolicited Lottery / UPI Prize Hook (High Risk)
```text
Congratulations! You have won ₹25,000 cash reward from PhonePe Rewards. Click here to claim into your account: http://tinyurl.com/claim-reward-upi and enter your 6-digit UPI PIN.
```

### Scenario C: Legitimate Banking Alert (Low Risk)
```text
Your account XXXXXX7812 has been credited with INR 75,000.00 on 01-Feb-2025 by NEFT from TechCorp Solutions. Available balance INR 1,15,000.00.
```
